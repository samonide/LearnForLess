About These Directions

A. Editorial List 推荐
Foreman-inspired single-column layout. Courses as entries in a reading list. Bracketed section labels. Progress inline, not in cards. Typography carries the structure. Best embodies the "content as design" principle.
B. Magazine Grid
Asymmetric editorial grid. Featured course gets prominence (2/3 width), secondary courses compact. Large typographic numbers. Most visual variety; risks feeling busier than the other directions.
C. Timeline
Chapters with numbered markers. Learning as a journey metaphor. Each course is a chapter with progress as a chapter marker. Narrative feel. Works well for sequential learning.
D. Minimalist
Stripped to essentials. Monospace numbering, single-line entries, minimal chrome. Most radical departure from the current card grid. Strong personality but less warm; risks feeling cold for an education platform.

Recommendation: A — Editorial List best translates Foreman's compositional principles into an education context while maintaining warmth and usability. The single-column, typography-led structure creates a distinctive reading experience that doesn't feel like a SaaS template.
